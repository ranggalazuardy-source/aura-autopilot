# AURA Brand Identity

## Brand
- Name: AURA
- Expanded name: Autonomous Unified Revenue Agent
- Positioning: a guarded, multi-channel revenue operations system for legitimate creator, affiliate and owned-media businesses.
- Promise: one operating hub for discovering, testing and tracking revenue opportunities with human control over spending and external commitments.
- Voice: clear, capable, transparent, practical; never promise guaranteed income.
- Tagline: Build once. Operate smarter. Grow responsibly.

## Handle naming candidates
Preferred candidate, subject to availability checks on each platform:
- `@aura.operations`
- `@auraautopilot`
- `@aura.revenue`

Use the same available handle everywhere where possible. Do not impersonate an existing company or use a platform trademark in a way that implies endorsement. Do not claim an unverified handle is reserved.

## Visual direction
- Primary: midnight navy `#10182B`
- Secondary: deep slate `#202C45`
- Accent: soft violet `#9B8CFF`
- Highlight: mint `#6EE7B7`
- Background: off-white `#F5F7FB`
- Style: minimal, technical, calm, high contrast; avoid crypto/ get-rich-quick visual clichés.

**YouTube:** Building smarter creator and affiliate operations. Experiments, systems and transparent results. No guaranteed-income claims.

## Account ownership rule
All accounts must be created under the user's real ownership and contact details. AURA is the brand/operator identity, not a fictional human. The owner retains primary recovery email, phone, passkeys/2FA and recovery codes.
