# AURA Clipper Workflow

## Scope
Support clipping as both a channel format and a potential paid editing service. The workflow must not assume that any public video is free to reuse.

## Intake
Capture:
- source file or URL
- source owner / client
- written permission or license reference
- permitted platforms, territories, duration, and commercial use
- requested number and length of clips
- brand/style instructions
- delivery deadline

## Processing
- transcribe and identify candidate moments
- rank by clarity, standalone context, useful insight, and hook strength
- create a proposed in/out timestamp and editorial rationale
- generate captions and a draft title/description
- render candidate versions through the approved clipper
- preserve source attribution when required by the license or agreement

## QA
- verify source permission covers editing and commercial distribution
- check no misleading edits or out-of-context statements
- verify names, claims, subtitles, and audio
- review platform-specific aspect ratio and safe areas
- check music and third-party visual rights
- require owner approval before delivery/publication

## Service offer hypothesis
Offer editing packages to creators, podcasters, educators, and businesses using media they own or have permission to provide. Start with a small sample and written scope. Do not promise virality or guaranteed views.

## Metrics
Track clips delivered, revision rate, editing time, client acceptance, on-time delivery, revenue, refunds, platform fees, and net margin.
