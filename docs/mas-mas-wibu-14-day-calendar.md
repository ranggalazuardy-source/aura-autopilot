# Mas Mas Wibu — 14-Day Starter Calendar

This is a proposed production queue. It does not mean any video has been created, uploaded, or scheduled.

## Editorial rules
- Focus on manhua discovery and original commentary.
- Verify title names, plot details, author/artist credits, and release status before recording.
- Avoid reproducing full chapters or using unlicensed panels/footage/music.
- Every clip must be rights-cleared and add meaningful original commentary.
- If a topic cannot pass the rights or fact check, replace it; do not publish just to meet a quota.

## Daily production workflow
1. Research: verify title, credits, premise, and current facts.
2. Outline: hook, context, one core insight, conclusion.
3. Rights check: confirm every visual and audio asset is usable.
4. Produce: voiceover or on-camera narration, captions, original graphics.
5. QA: spelling, audio clarity, pacing, claims, spoiler warning, thumbnail/title accuracy.
6. Approval: channel owner reviews before publishing.
7. Record: log URL, format, topic, publish date, and available performance metrics after publication.

## Weekly review
Compare each format after enough data accumulates. Prioritize retention, engaged views, subscribers gained, and repeatability. Views alone are insufficient. Pause any format that depends on unauthorized reposting.
