# Mas Mas Wibu — Channel Operating Strategy

Status: operating plan, not a claim of completed channel changes.

## Decision
Retain the existing Mas Mas Wibu YouTube channel. Do not create a replacement channel, delete existing uploads, or rename the channel until the owner explicitly approves a visual rebrand after reviewing the existing audience fit.

## Positioning
A practical entertainment channel for manhua discovery, reviews, recommendations, character/story discussions, and original short-form commentary.

Working promise: "Bantu penonton menemukan manhua menarik tanpa buang waktu."

## Content pillars
1. Manhua discovery and review — 50%
   - spoiler-light recommendations
   - premise, genre, strengths, caveats
   - who may enjoy the title
2. Manhua explainers and discussion — 30%
   - character analysis, themes, world-building, reading order when verified
   - theories clearly labeled as theories
3. Anime/manhua-adjacent clips with original contribution — 20%
   - only use footage/audio the channel owns, has permission to use, or can lawfully use
   - add substantial original narration, criticism, explanation, or analysis
   - do not assume credit, short duration, cropping, subtitles, or speed changes grant permission
   - avoid compilations or minimally edited reposts

The percentages are an initial 30-day experiment, not a conclusion from analytics.

## Production format
- Shorts: 20–45 seconds, one idea per video, hook in the first 1–2 seconds, concise explanation, clear ending.
- Long-form: 4–8 minutes when a topic warrants deeper review; no padding.
- Language: natural Indonesian, conversational and clear.
- Visuals: original graphics, licensed/authorized art, screenshots only where permitted, and creator-made diagrams. Avoid presenting copyrighted panels as a substitute for the original work.

## Repeatable short script
1. Hook: a specific question or strong premise.
2. Context: identify the title/topic and avoid unsupported claims.
3. Value: one review point, explanation, or recommendation.
4. Qualification: mention spoilers or uncertainty when relevant.
5. CTA: ask a focused question or invite viewers to a related video.

## Quality and rights checklist
- Is the script original and materially transformative?
- Are footage, panels, music, and images owned, licensed, permissioned, or otherwise lawfully usable?
- Does the video add clear commentary, criticism, education, or analysis rather than merely repackaging?
- Are title, creator, source, and facts accurate?
- Is any spoiler clearly signposted?
- Are claims about monetization or copyright not overstated?

Fair use/fair dealing and platform copyright outcomes are fact-specific; this checklist is not legal advice. YouTube monetization review can assess reused content separately from copyright claims.

## Publishing guardrails
- Prepare drafts and metadata automatically.
- Require owner approval before public upload, scheduling, deletion, changing channel identity, or responding to sensitive comments.
- No fake engagement, sub-for-sub, view buying, spam, misleading thumbnails, or unauthorized reposting.
- Do not promise revenue or monetization eligibility.

## First 30-day operating cadence
- Publish one Short per day only when a rights-cleared, quality-checked draft is ready.
- Publish one deeper review every 1–2 weeks if research and rights checks are complete.
- Review performance weekly; retain winning topics and formats, not just isolated viral spikes.
- Track views, engaged views, average view duration, retention, viewed-vs-swiped where available, subscribers gained, comments, and returning viewers.

## Initial success criteria
Establish a baseline over the first 14 days. Compare formats using retention and subscriber conversion, not views alone. Do not declare a winner from a single viral post.
