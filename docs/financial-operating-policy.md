# AURA Financial Operating Policy

This policy implements the owner-approved configuration in `config/aura.toml`.

## Approved operating settings
- Initial capital: Rp0.
- Risk profile: moderate.
- Distribution: 50% of reconciled net distributable cash to the owner; 50% retained for reinvestment.
- Reporting and closing: monthly, on the 1st day of the month, Asia/Jakarta time.
- Spending: every expense, subscription, API charge, advertisement, purchase, or contractor cost requires explicit owner approval first.
- Financial transactions: every trade, investment purchase/sale, payment, transfer, or other financial transaction requires explicit owner approval first.
- AI wallet: not permitted. Funds remain in accounts legally owned and controlled by the owner.
- Agent withdrawals/transfers: disabled.

## Monthly calculation
Net distributable cash is calculated only after reconciling cash actually received and deducting:
1. platform and payment processing fees;
2. refunds, chargebacks and outstanding liabilities;
3. approved operating expenses;
4. owner-approved tax and operating reserves.

The resulting positive amount is split:
- 50% proposed owner distribution;
- 50% retained business reinvestment.

If net distributable cash is zero or negative, no payout is proposed. Unrealized investment gains, views, clicks, pending invoices, and forecasts are not distributable cash.

## Approval workflow
AURA prepares a proposal with amount, purpose, counterparty/venue, downside, fees, and impact. It waits for explicit owner approval before spending or transacting. Silence is not approval. An approval for one transaction does not authorize later transactions.

## Monthly report
On the 1st of each month, prepare the previous month's report with:
- opening and closing cash balances by owner-connected account;
- receipts by revenue stream;
- expenses and fees;
- refunds and liabilities;
- realized investment profit/loss;
- unrealized investment valuation changes (separate);
- tax and operating reserves;
- net distributable cash;
- proposed 50/50 distribution and reinvestment amounts;
- evidence links and unreconciled exceptions.

AURA may prepare the report and remind the owner. It cannot initiate bank/e-wallet withdrawals or transfers. The owner completes the transfer in their own official financial account.

## Risk controls
Moderate is a portfolio risk preference, not permission to trade. Live equity and crypto execution remain disabled. Paper trading and research are allowed. Before any live trading can be considered, the owner must separately approve venue, instrument universe, per-trade and daily loss caps, maximum drawdown, position sizing, fees/slippage assumptions, and emergency stop.
