# AURA Agent Registry

All agents are logical roles within one orchestrated system. They are not separate legal persons or bank-account holders.

| Agent | Primary responsibility | Initial autonomy | Skill pack | Key output |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO Orchestrator | Assign work and prioritize opportunities | L1 | platform-engineering-security; market-intelligence | Daily portfolio priorities |
| Market Intelligence | Discover markets, trends, buyers and competitors | L1 | market-intelligence | Sourced opportunity briefs |
| Content & Media | Create original content and publishing packages | L2 | growth-content-commerce | Approved-ready content |
| Clipping Studio | Use owner's clipper for authorized media | L2 | growth-content-commerce | Clip candidates and metadata |
| Affiliate Commerce | Product and offer research | L1/L2 | growth-content-commerce | Compliant affiliate plans |
| E-commerce / Dropship | Supplier, margin and fulfilment validation | L1/L2 | growth-content-commerce | Unit economics and test plan |
| Service Sales | Prospect research and proposal drafts | L1/L2 | growth-content-commerce | Qualified lead pipeline |
| Digital Products | Validate and draft digital products | L2 | growth-content-commerce | Product prototypes |
| SEO / Website | Build owned-media plans and assets | L2 | growth-content-commerce | SEO pages and traffic reports |
| Equity Research | Research listed securities | L1 | finance-investment-risk | Research notes and paper portfolio |
| Crypto Research | Research eligible digital assets | L1 | finance-investment-risk | Risk-rated research notes |
| Trading Risk Manager | Independently review exposure and risk | Veto | finance-investment-risk | Risk decision and limits |
| Finance & Treasury | Reconcile accounts and calculate distributions | L1 | finance-investment-risk | Monthly close and payout proposal |
| Compliance & Rights | Policy, legal, rights and disclosure checks | Veto | all packs; policy veto | Pass / revise / reject |
| QA / Auditor | Verify evidence, code and agent results | Veto | platform-engineering-security; all pack audits | Audit trail and exceptions |

## Agent communication contract
Every task carries: task ID, owner, objective, evidence/source links, assumptions, cost, risk, legal/policy review, status, result, and next action.

Agents cannot grant themselves permissions. Only the owner can authorize new scopes, financial permissions, spending, public publication, and external commitments.
