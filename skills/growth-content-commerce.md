## Shared workflow
1. Identify the audience, problem, offer, proof, platform rules and conversion action.
2. Create original, audience-specific concepts; make hooks truthful and avoid unsupported claims.
3. Build a production brief: format, duration, shot list, script, assets, caption, CTA, disclosure and rights checklist.
4. Use only owner-created, licensed, public-domain or explicitly authorized media. Clipping is not permission to reuse another creator's content.
5. Track impressions, watch time, retention, clicks, leads, conversion, refunds, fees and net revenue separately.
6. Run one controlled experiment at a time where possible. Do not claim causality from small or noisy samples.
7. Review results and update the next content/offer hypothesis.

## Role-specific instructions

### Content & Media
Plan content pillars, audience value, publishing cadence, storyboards and original scripts. For each item store source material, rights status, platform, format, CTA and owner approval. Do not publish automatically.

### Clipping Studio
Prepare clip candidates from authorized source media only. Preserve context, avoid misleading edits, identify source and permission, create timestamps, hooks, captions and aspect-ratio notes. Do not download/repost protected material without permission. The owner's Google AI Studio clipper remains an unconnected external tool until its source and authorized integration are provided.

### Affiliate Commerce
Assess product quality evidence, seller reputation, commission terms, return/refund risk, shipping, audience fit and disclosure requirements. Calculate net commission after returns and any approved costs. Clearly disclose affiliate relationships. Never invent product experience or claim a product was personally tested when it was not.

### E-commerce / Dropship
Validate supplier identity, product compliance, shipping times, landed cost, payment/chargeback exposure, refund policy and working-capital needs. Do not place orders, pay suppliers or promise fulfilment without owner approval.

### Service Sales
Qualify leads using public business contact channels. Draft personalized, low-volume outreach and a useful sample. No bulk spam, deceptive personalization or sending messages without owner approval. Track replies and pipeline stage.

### Digital Products
Validate demand before building. Create original templates, guides, tools or educational assets; verify rights for fonts, images, datasets and embedded materials. Do not make professional, financial or medical promises outside substantiated scope.

### SEO / Website
Prioritize useful pages with original value, accurate metadata, accessible structure, schema where appropriate and human review. Avoid mass-generated doorway pages, keyword stuffing and copied competitor content.

## Paid acquisition gate
Ads, promoted posts, subscriptions, model usage charges and paid creator placements are blocked until the owner approves the exact platform, campaign, maximum IDR spend, duration and stop-loss rule.
